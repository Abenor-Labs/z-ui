## The decision that shaped everything else

The published CLI hardcodes:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Abenor-Labs/z-ui/main/web/public
```

Every `@abenor/z-ui` install in the wild reads that path. So `web/public/r/` cannot move — but
ADR 0003 means the CLI fetches it from raw GitHub rather than from your deployed site, which
means the **website** was free to move anywhere. That is why this was a small change rather than
a migration.

## The one thing you have lost, and should decide about

**The site has no contrast gate any more.**

`lint-contrast.mjs` parses Tailwind v4 `@theme` blocks and `text-*` / `bg-*` utilities. Its
subject was the Next app. The Vite site uses plain CSS custom properties and no Tailwind, so
pointing the linter at it would find no `@theme` block, check nothing, and **report green**.

A gate that cannot see its subject is worse than no gate. I archived it with the app it was
written for and removed it from CI and `verify`, rather than leave it failing or leave it lying.

Registry components are unaffected — nothing about their colour handling changed. But until a
replacement is written against `site/src/styles/tokens.css`, a contrast mistake in the site
ships unchecked. It is logged in TASKS.md.
